Slide 02 The Problem

The Crisis in Indian Agriculture

  • Excessive Water Waste: Traditional farming consumes 70% of India's freshwater. Flood irrigation wastes up to 60% of water through evaporation and runoff.
  • Toxic Pesticide Overuse: India uses 0.29 kg/hectare of pesticides. Residues found in 20–30% of retail produce exceed safe limits, causing chronic health issues.
  • Shrinking Arable Land: India loses ~1.5 million hectares of farmland annually to urbanization, leaving fewer resources for a growing 1.4 billion population.
  • Unpredictable Supply Chains: Seasonal monsoon dependency causes 30–40% price fluctuations. Climate change makes harvests increasingly unreliable.
  • Zero Traceability: Consumers cannot verify origin, growing methods, or chemical exposure of produce sold in mandis and retail markets.
  • Post-Harvest Losses: India loses ₹92,000 Crore worth of produce annually (~16% of output) due to lack of cold chain infrastructure.

Slide 04 Our Solution

Precision NFT Hydroponics

  • Nutrient Film Technique (NFT): Closed-loop recirculating system growing 78,000+ lettuce plants on 1 acre — 10x yield per sq.m vs traditional farming.
  • Custom Modular Aluminum Frames: 40×40mm T-Slot profiles (3m × 1.5m × 1m sets) designed for vertical expansion — add a 2nd tier without rebuilding.
  • Climate-Controlled Polyhouse: Fan-and-pad cooling, 24 HAF circulation fans, 832 fogger nozzles, and automated pH/EC controllers for year-round production.
  • Hybrid Light Strategy: Natural sunlight primary + supplemental LEDs only on lower tiers — 70% less LED cost than indoor vertical farms.
  • 90% Water Savings: Gravity-fed closed-loop with underground collection sumps, RO purification (1000 LPH), and nutrient recycling.

USP — What Makes Us Unique

  • Vertically Expandable NFT: First modular aluminum frame system designed to double capacity by stacking — no competitor offers this.
  • Polyhouse + Hydroponics Hybrid: Natural-light-first approach cuts energy costs by 70% vs indoor farms while maintaining controlled environment quality.
